## A. UNIQLO Masterpiece 2026 Fall/Winter — Merino Sweater（US）
- URL: https://www.uniqlo.com/us/en/contents/feature/masterpiece/product/merino-crew-neck-sweater/
- 访问日期: 2026-10-13（WebFetch）
- 页面标题含 "UNIQLO Masterpiece 2026 Fall / Winter"
- 原文：
  > "This wool is treated with shrink‑resistant and anti‑pilling finishes, while preserving its delicate texture."
  > "With a special non-shrinking treatment, this sweater is machine washable while retaining the elegant feel of wool."
  > "After 15 machine washes, as shown, there's no noticeable pilling or fuzz."
  > 脚注: "Tested by Kaken Test Center using JIS L1930 standards with a pulsator‑type washing machine. Garments were ironed after washing."
  > "Extra Fine Merino is an extremely thin fiber, approximately 1/4 the size of a human hair."
  > "After washing, reshape and dry flat."
- 同类页面的 SG 版 https://www.uniqlo.com/sg/en/contents/feature/masterpiece/product/merino-crew-neck-sweater/ 在 2026-10-13 WebFetch 只读到品牌口号，**未读到**测试脚注。
- 搜索摘要（未直接打开）称 AU 版写作 "Type C (pulsator) washing machine … following the JIS L1930 testing method"：https://www.uniqlo.com/au/en/contents/feature/masterpiece/product/merino-crew-neck-sweater/

**解读**：
- 官方披露了：测试机构（一般财团法人 KAKEN/ケケン試験認証センター）、方法标准（JIS L1930 家庭洗涤试验方法）、机型（波轮式，即 JIS 的 C 法）、次数（15 次）、"洗后熨烫"。
- 官方**未披露**：具体洗涤程序编号（如 C4N/C4M）、温度、干燥方式、**尺寸变化率数值**（JIS L1930 本身只是洗涤程序标准，尺寸变化率通常另按 JIS L1909 判定；Uniqlo 未给出）、抗起球等级（如 JIS L1076 等级）。
- "Garments were ironed after washing"——熨烫会把部分缩水/变形拉回，因此宣传照的效果可能好于消费者不熨烫的实际情况。置信度（官方确有此说法）：高；（该说法代表真实耐洗性）：中。
