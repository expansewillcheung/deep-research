# US rare earth government financing disbursements and US demand data: open gaps (as of 22 Oct 2026)

Method note: 25 search/fetch calls used (limit reached). Many search-result summaries are secondary; items marked "unverified" were not confirmed in a primary document. Core finding: none of the OSC loans below is shown to have closed or disbursed in any source found.

## Q1. Have the OSC loans, USAR CHIPS funding, Serra Verde SPV and Project Vault closed or disbursed?

### Takeaway
No source found shows a closed or disbursed OSC loan for Vulcan, ReElement, Energy Fuels or Phoenix Tailings. ReElement's $80M was dropped (Reuters, 10 Jul 2026). Energy Fuels ($725M) and Phoenix Tailings ($500M) are conditional commitments only. Project Vault has no quantified rare earth purchases. USAR's CHIPS money is milestone-based and no disbursed amount was found.

### Cited Findings
**Vulcan Elements ($620M OSC loan)**
- The Pentagon announced in Nov 2025 the $620M OSC loan, with $50M Commerce CHIPS incentive/equity and about $550M private capital; the loan was funded from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act appropriation. — [Wikipedia: Vulcan Elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulcan_Elements); [Axios, 18 Nov 2025](https://www.axios.com/local/raleigh/2025/11/18/rare-earth-magnet-maker-plans-1000-job-factory-in-north-carolina)
- Reuters (10 Jul 2026), citing administration officials: Vulcan's $620M loan remains "on track". The article does not say it has closed or been disbursed. — [Reuters via WMBD, 10 Jul 2026](https://wmbdradio.com/2026/07/10/rare-earths-startup-reelement-no-longer-seeking-80-million-pentagon-loan-administration-sources-say/)
- A Cryptopolitan article of 3 Dec 2025 says the deal was "finalized last month" (Nov 2025) and gives no disbursement information. This is loose wording; the Reuters July 2026 "on track" language suggests the loan had not been closed. — [Cryptopolitan, 3 Dec 2025](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/trump-rare-earths-company-pentagon-620m/)
- Axios (23 Sep 2026) says Vulcan "late last year secured" the $620M OSC loan. The Benson, NC plant is expected to begin initial operations in 2027. Vulcan is supplying magnets for Army drones. — [Axios, 23 Sep 2026](https://axios.com/2026/09/23/vulcan-magnets-army-drones-skyfoundry)
- Controversy: ProPublica/NPR (28 May 2026) reported the White House asked the Pentagon to lend to Vulcan, whose investor 1789 Capital is linked to Donald Trump Jr.; the Pentagon and company deny favoritism. — [NPR/Texas Public Radio, 28 May 2026](https://www.tpr.org/2026-05-28/white-house-asked-pentagon-to-loan-money-to-a-company-linked-to-trumps-oldest-son)

**ReElement ($80M OSC loan)**
- The $80M and $620M loans were part of a $700M conditional OSC package announced in Nov 2025 (Argus gives the announcement date as 21 Nov). — [Argus Media](https://www.argusmedia.com/es/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2757859-us-loans-vulcan-reelement-700mn-to-make-magnets)

**Energy Fuels ($725M OSC)**

The term is 20 years, the funds go to White Mesa Mill expansion and a planned US rare earth metals/alloy facility, and it is subject to due diligence, definitive agreements and approvals. — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/energy-fuels-receives-conditional-725-million-financing-commitment-from-u-s-government/); [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/?p=1208822); [Breaking Defense, Jun 2026](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/06/pentagon-inks-pair-of-rare-earth-mineral-loans-for-1-2-billion/)
- The Energy Fuels Q2 2026 10-Q still describes it as a conditional commitment, and the company says final terms and timing will follow definitive documentation. No closing found. — [Energy Fuels 10-Q (period ending 30 Jun 2026)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001385849/000138584926000029/efr-20260630.htm) (read via search summary only)

**Phoenix Tailings ($500M OSC)**

- Disbursement to date across all items above is likely very small or zero, but this is not confirmed by any primary source.

## Q3. US demand: total NdFeB tonnes, sectoral split, DoD Dy/Tb requirement, embedded magnets

### Takeaway
No official (USGS, DOE, Commerce, DoD) estimate was found. The best available numbers are secondary: about 48,000 t of US demand for the most common magnet type (2025, unsourced) and a Pentagon need of 3,000-4,000 t/yr rising to about 10,000 t by 2030 (secondary). Commerce is quoted as saying up to 60% of US NdFeB demand arrives embedded in imported goods.

### Cited Findings
- OilPrice (18 Oct 2026): US demand about 48,000 t of the most common rare earth magnet (for 2025), domestic supply about 300 t, US magnet capacity on track for about 5,000 t by end-2026. The article names no source (links to Mining.com) and gives no sector split. — [OilPrice, 18 Oct 2026](https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/America-Needs-48000-Tons-of-Rare-Earth-Magnets-It-Only-Makes-300.amp.html)
- Pentagon need for specialized rare earth magnets is about 3,000-4,000 t/yr now, rising to about 10,000 t by 2030. This is secondary reporting, unattributed to a DoD document in the snippet. — [Mining Stock Education, Apr 2026](https://www.miningstockeducation.com/2026/04/3-rare-earth-metals-keeping-pentagon-planners-up-at-night/)
- The DFARS ban on China-linked NdFeB and SmCo magnets in Pentagon procurement takes effect January 2027. — same source and [OilPrice](https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/America-Needs-48000-Tons-of-Rare-Earth-Magnets-It-Only-Makes-300.amp.html)
- Up to 60% of essential US civilian NdFeB demand is met through embedded imports (attributed to a Commerce estimate by Rare Earth Exchanges, 13 Sep 2026; the original Commerce document was not seen). — [Rare Earth Exchanges, 13 Sep 2026](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/us-ndfeb-magnet-supply-chain-china/) (paywalled; highlights only)
- Section 232 on processed critical minerals: the proclamation of 14 Jan 2026 directed negotiations with a 180-day window (to about mid-Jul 2026). Commerce found the US fully import-dependent for 12 critical minerals and 50%+ dependent for 29 others. No magnet tonnage or sector split was found. — [Thomson Reuters, 15 Jan 2026](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/onesource-global-trade/regulatory-insights/2026/january-15th/trump-administration-directs-negotiations-on-criti); [Morgan Lewis, Feb 2026](https://www.morganlewis.com/ru/pubs/2026/02/section-232-investigations-prompt-trade-negotiation)
- Heavy RE supply scale: MP Materials' heavy rare earth plant about 200 t/yr Dy+Tb (secondary); SRC Saskatchewan up to 30 t Dy oxide and 15 t Tb oxide a year. — [Mining Stock Education, Apr 2026](https://www.miningstockeducation.com/2026/04/3-rare-earth-metals-keeping-pentagon-planners-up-at-night/)
- The earlier (2022) Section 232 NdFeB investigation concluded without tariffs. — [Mayer Brown, 29 Sep 2022](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2022/09/biden-administration-concludes-section-232-investigation-into-imports-of-ndfeb-magnets-but-does-not-impose-tariffs)

### Inferences
- The 48,000 t figure is probably total or broadly defined magnet demand and should not be treated as an official NdFeB-only number. It is roughly 5.8x the 2025 direct-import tonnage of HS 850511 below, consistent with much demand being embedded in imported goods or the figures not being like-for-like.
- Treat the DoD 3,000-4,000 t/yr figure with caution: it is large relative to typical defense magnet use and its basis is unknown.

### Gaps
- No official DoD annual Dy/Tb requirement (tonnes) found.
- No official sectoral split of US magnet demand.
- No quantification of magnets embedded in imported components beyond the "up to 60%" claim.
- USGS MCS 2026 rare earths chapter, the DOE critical materials assessment and the Commerce 232 report were not directly fetched.

## Q4. US NdFeB magnet imports (HTS 850511 / 8505.11.0070) 2024, 2025, 2026 YTD

### Takeaway
Rare Earth Exchanges (13 Sep 2026) reports China's share of US direct magnet imports rose from 75.3% (2024) to 85.1% (2025) as total imports fell. The tonnage figures below come from a search-engine summary of the paywalled article and have not been checked against Census or USITC data. No 2026 YTD figure and no 8505.11.0070-specific series were found.

### Cited Findings
- HS 850511 (all metal permanent magnets, not NdFeB only): about 9,407 t in 2024 and 8,321 t in 2025. China about 7,079 t (2024, 75.3%) and about 7,081 t (2025, 85.1%). The share figures (75.3% and 85.1%) were verified on the article page; the tonnages come from the search summary only. — [Rare Earth Exchanges, 13 Sep 2026](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/us-ndfeb-magnet-supply-chain-china/)
- Adamas Intelligence (Chinese export-side data): US imports from China of nearly 7,500 t in 2024, a different measure from US import data. — [Adamas Intelligence](https://www.adamasintel.com/ja/record-rare-earth-magnet-exports-from-china-in-2024/)
- US imports of sintered NdFeB magnets fell 44% in April 2025 to 430 t (a partial-year datapoint). — [SMM, Jun 2025](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103368730)

### Inferences
- The tonnages are internally consistent: 7,081 / 8,321 = 85.1%, and 7,079 / 9,407 = 75.3%. China's absolute tonnage was flat while non-China sources fell from about 2,330 t to about 1,240 t.

### Gaps
- No Census / USITC DataWeb pull for 8505.11.0070 for 2024, 2025 or 2026 YTD. Those sites were not queried directly and the call limit was reached.
- No 2026 YTD tonnage or China share.
- The tonnage numbers for HS 850511 rest on a secondary source and a search summary; flag as unverified.
