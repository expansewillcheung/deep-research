## MP Materials: heavy REE (Dy/Tb) circuit, Independence, 10X, PPA, Q3 2026 date

### Takeaway
The Dy/Tb circuit is still being commissioned as of the Q2 2026 call (6 Aug 2026), with shipments to Independence guided for "later this year". The 200 t/yr Dy/Tb nameplate and 3,000 t/yr feed figures come from the Q3 2025 release; I found no 2026 update. PPA income was $17.6M in Q2 2026 and $59.9M for 1H26. The Q3 2026 results are due 5 Nov 2026, so they are not out yet.

### Cited Findings
- Dy/Tb circuit design: about 3,000 MT/yr of feedstock and initial focus on Dy and Tb. Nameplate is 200 MT/yr Dy/Tb, meant to support the planned 10,000 MT/yr NdFeB magnet output. Commissioning was planned for mid-2026. This is a search-tool summary of the Q3 2025 release (6 Nov 2025). [MP Q3 2025 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136825000052/mpmcq32025er.htm); [PDF release](https://s25.q4cdn.com/570172628/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/MP-Materials-Q3-2025-Earnings-Release-FINAL.pdf). I did not read the original text directly.
- Feed source: MP has stockpiled heavy rare earth concentrate ("SEG+") at Mountain Pass since late 2023 and plans to process third-party feeds. Samarium is planned as the next product after Dy/Tb. Source as above (same search summary).
- Q2 2026 status (release 6 Aug 2026): management said it is "actively commissioning" the Dy/Tb circuit and still expects to start shipping product from Mountain Pass to Independence "later this year". This is from a search summary of the Q2 call and release. [MP Q2 2026 release](https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-reports-second-quarter-2026-results); [Q2 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001801368/000180136826000047/mpmcq22026er.htm). The 8-K text itself only mentions "ability to expand its heavy rare earth separation capabilities", with no capacity or date.
- First-production date for Dy/Tb: not confirmed. As of 6 Aug 2026 the circuit was still in commissioning. I found nothing after that.
- Q2 2026 NdPr production was 840 MT (+41% y/y) and NdPr sales 1,006 MT. Revenue was $108.5M and the net loss $20.3M. The Magnetics segment had $16.5M revenue and $7.5M Adj. EBITDA. [MP Q2 2026 release](https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-reports-second-quarter-2026-results) (via search summary).
- Independence (Fort Worth): the Q2 2026 10-Q says it "commenced manufacturing of neodymium-iron-boron ("NdFeB") permanent magnets in December 2025". [MP 10-Q 30 Jun 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001801368/000180136826000048/mp-20260630.htm). The Q4 2025 release says first NdFeB magnets were produced on commercial equipment at Independence. [MP Q4 2025 release](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/MP-Materials-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results/). Q2 2026 said magnet qualification "advanced through additional customer and regulatory test deliveries" ([Q2 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001801368/000180136826000047/mpmcq22026er.htm)).
- Actual 2026 Independence magnet tonnage: not disclosed in the Q2 10-Q or Q2 8-K text I read. The only proxy is Magnetics segment revenue of $16.5M in Q2 2026, which includes precursor product sales (Q4 2025 release said segment EBITDA was driven by precursor sales).
- 10X facility (Northlake, TX): construction has commenced. MP bought a 120-acre site in April 2026 for about $80M. The DoW guarantees at least $140M annual EBITDA (subject to escalation) and is obligated to buy all magnets produced. [MP 10-Q 30 Jun 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001801368/000180136826000048/mp-20260630.htm). The 10-Q excerpt I read states no tonnage. The Q4 2025 release mentions a $200M incentive package for 10X. The 10,000 MT/yr total magnet target is tied to the Dy/Tb circuit in the Q3 2025 summary above. No primary-source statement of 10X-only capacity was found.
- PPA mechanics: the price floor is $110/kg NdPr, starting 1 Oct 2025, covering NdPr produced at Mountain Pass and sold or stockpiled from Q4 2025. [MP 10-Q 30 Jun 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001801368/000180136826000048/mp-20260630.htm); [Q4 2025 release](https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results).
- PPA income Q2 2026: $17,580K. Six months 2026: $59,853K. Gross upfront PPA asset is $221.1M, with $22.1M amortized in 1H26. 2025 comparison periods show no PPA income. [MP 10-Q 30 Jun 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001801368/000180136826000048/mp-20260630.htm).
- PPA income Q4 2025: about $51M, from an earnings-call summary only (not verified in the press release; the same summary says collection on stockpiles is discretionary). Q4 2025 net income was $9.4M, Adj. EBITDA $39.2M, and $11.4M of PPA amortization was noted. [MP Q4 2025 release](https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results). Cross-check: 1H26 PPA of $59.9M less Q2 $17.6M implies Q1 2026 of about $42.3M (my arithmetic).
- Q3 2026 earnings: Thursday 5 Nov 2026, after the US close, call at 5:00pm ET. Announcement dated 8 Oct 2026. [MP IR](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/MP-Materials-Announces-Date-for-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-and-Webcast/default.aspx). Bullstory shows 4 Nov, which conflicts with the company, so use 5 Nov. Results are not yet out.

### Inferences
- The Dy/Tb nameplate (200 t/yr) is a design figure and is not producing at scale as of 22 Oct 2026. The report should say "commissioning, shipments guided for late 2026".
- Q3 2026 PPA income will likely be reported on 5 Nov. A report dated 22 Oct should treat Q3 as pending.

### Gaps
- Independence NdPr metal capacity (the question names it): not found. Likely about 1,000 t/yr NdPr metal in earlier MP guidance, but I have no source in this session, so do not use it.
- Actual 2026 magnet tonnage at Independence: not disclosed in the sources read.
- Any dated Dy/Tb first-production announcement after Aug 2026: none found. The Q2 call transcript (Benzinga) returned HTTP 403.
- The 10-Q HREE note (Note 15) was truncated and not read.
- 10X nameplate confirmed by a primary source: not found.

## Energy Fuels: NdPr production, ASM Korean plant, VAC closing

### Takeaway
No NdPr oxide production figure for 2025 or 2026 YTD was found in the sources read. The White Mesa light REE circuit nameplate is 850-1,000 tpa NdPr. VAC was signed on 23 Jun 2026 and had not closed; secondary reports point to early 2027. ASM was targeted to close by end-August 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Light REE circuit capacity: 850-1,000 tpa NdPr. The heavy REE expansion (construction began 29 Jul 2026) adds up to about 20 tpa Tb and 120 tpa Dy oxide. A separate Phase 2 is planned as early as 2029 subject to approvals and feed. [Energy Fuels via search summary of company news](https://www.cruxinvestor.com/posts/energy-fuels-white-mesa-mill-produces-first-us-terbium-oxide-in-decades-targets-commercial-heavy-rare-earth-output-by-2027); [Q2 2026 results](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3673264/energy-fuels-announces-q2-2026-results).
- Phase 1B heavy expansion is $104M, targeting commercial-scale Tb/Dy separation by late 2027. A conditional $725M OSC loan commitment supports expansions. [Q2 2026 results](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3673264/energy-fuels-announces-q2-2026-results).
- First kilogram of Tb oxide (99.9%) was produced at White Mesa in March 2026 on a pilot scale. Earlier, nearly 30 kg of 99.9% Dy oxide was produced. [Cruxinvestor](https://www.cruxinvestor.com/posts/energy-fuels-white-mesa-mill-produces-first-us-terbium-oxide-in-decades-targets-commercial-heavy-rare-earth-output-by-2027).
- VAC: definitive agreement signed 23 Jun 2026 for about $1.9B in cash and stock (reported structure: $718M cash plus 65.853M new shares). The Sumter, SC facility is described as 2,000 tpa, scalable to 12,000 tpa. The Q2 results release gives no closing date; other sources point to early 2027. [Q2 2026 results](https://finviz.com/news/377973/energy-fuels-announces-q2-2026-results); [10-Q 30 Jun 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001385849/000138584926000029/efr-20260630.htm); [search summary on VAC timing](https://www.mining-technology.com/newsletters/energy-fuels-agreement-buy-vac-1-9bn). The early-2027 timing is a secondary-source claim; the 10-Q lists VAC as proposed.
- ASM: Q2 release says closing was expected by the end of August 2026, subject to court, regulatory and shareholder approvals; Australian FIRB approval was obtained in Q2. [Q2 2026 results](https://finviz.com/news/377973/energy-fuels-announces-q2-2026-results). ASM securityholders voted in favour of the schemes ([Ground News](https://ground.news/article/asm-securityholders-vote-in-favour-of-schemes)). A sidebar headline on Finviz, "Energy Fuels Completes Acquisition of Australian Strategic Materials" (28 Aug), is only a headline; I did not read the article, so completion is plausible but unconfirmed.

### Inferences
- With 850-1,000 tpa nameplate but no reported volumes, the report should not state actual NdPr output. Energy Fuels' NdPr output is probably small relative to nameplate (Phase 1 ramp), but this is unverified.

### Gaps
- Actual NdPr oxide production in 2025 and 2026 YTD: not found. The Q2 2026 release as fetched did not include it; the 10-Q MD&A was not read in full.
- ASM Korean Metals Plant (KMP) capacity: not found in sources read.
- Primary-source VAC closing date or confirmation: not found; treat as not closed as of the latest sources.
- Primary confirmation of ASM completion: not read.

### Cited Findings

Machine A is partly funded by an US$18.4M DoW agreement. [Ucore engineering report release](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/299208). Products focus on Sm/Eu/Gd and Tb/Dy, with NdPr also possible.
- Timing: H2 2026 commissioning and "early production" readiness (Stockwatch item); a Skillings article gives mid-2026 Phase 1 of 2,000 tpa TREO, which conflicts with the 600 tpa framing. [Ucore Louisiana 2026](https://ucore.com/?p=5895); [Skillings](https://skillings.net/ucore-louisiana-facility-supply-chain-milestone-timeline-and-risks). No first-production announcement found.

- ReElement (Marion, IN): $25M DoW investment reported July 2026 for equipment, lines and working capital; Transition Equity Partners provided a $200M strategic equity facility. Chromatographic separation of mined and recycled feeds. [Pulse 2](https://pulse2.com/reelement-technologies-raises-25-million-from-u-s-department-of-war-to-expand-rare-earth-refining/amp/); [Mining Digital](https://miningdigital.com/news/reelement-tep-securing-200m-rare-earth-refining). ## Lynas Seadrift, Texas and Lynas Sept 2026 quarterly

### Takeaway
Seadrift is officially dead. Lynas's FY2026 annual report (ASX, 26 Aug 2026) says funds "previously intended for the construction of a HRE facility in Texas" are reallocated, and that the facility "will no longer proceed". The Sept 2026 quarterly is not out (due about 29 Oct).

### Cited Findings
- Annual report text: "Lynas signed a binding Letter of Intent with the U.S. Government in March 2026 for the supply of approximately US$96 million of light and heavy rare earth oxides over a 4 year period... Funding for the supply agreement has been reallocated from funds previously intended for the construction of a HRE facility in Texas which will no longer proceed. The floor price for supply of NdPr oxide will be US$110/kg." [Lynas FY2026 Annual Report, ASX 26 Aug 2026](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260826/pdf/0737nx07s3tnf7.pdf) (text extracted from the PDF locally).
- Lynas first produced Sm oxide in Malaysia in March 2026, its third separated heavy REE. The same report announces an expanded HRE separation facility at Lynas Malaysia. Same annual report.
- FY2026 results: revenue A$977.9M, NPAT A$222.4M, oxide production 13,089 t (+25%), cash A$1,209.1M, no dividend. [Fool Australia, 26 Aug 2026](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/08/26/lynas-rare-earths-posts-record-fy26-profit-and-revenue-as-growth-ramps-up/); [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104081259-lynas-reports-strong-fy2026-results-with-revenue-at-9779m-driven-by-higher-rare-earth-prices).
