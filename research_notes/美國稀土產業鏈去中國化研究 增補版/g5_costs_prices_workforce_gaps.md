# G5 gap-closing: rare earth production costs, magnet prices, supply-demand balance, workforce (as of 22 Oct 2026)

Method note: 25-call budget fully used. "Verified" = I read the primary document text. "Search-summary only" = figure came from a search-result summary and I did not open the underlying page; the report writer should treat it as lower confidence. Several search results were secondary or undated, and I flag them.

## 1. Cost curves: all-in / opex per kg NdPr, Western producers vs Chinese separators

### Takeaway
No Western producer publicly reports a clean "all-in cost per kg NdPr" in its 2026 results. The only study-based figures are Energy Fuels (about US$29/kg, feedstock-dependent) and Arafura (about US$24-34/kg, 2019-2021 vintage studies). MP and Lynas disclose only aggregate cost of sales. I found no public per-kg cost for Northern Rare Earth or Shenghe, and no current Iluka per-kg NdPr figure. Third-party "incentive price" estimates for ex-China supply are US$75-105/kg.

### Cited Findings
- **Lynas (verified, FY26 results released 26 Aug 2026):** Cost of sales A$585.5m (FY25: A$426.7m). Total REO sales 12,122 t, NdPr sales 7,337 t. Record average selling price A$80.7/kg REO. Cost rise attributed to "higher fixed cost attributed to new facilities... in commissioning and ramp up", higher cost of sourcing inputs outside China, and geopolitical cost escalation. Lynas did NOT report a unit cost per kg. — [Lynas FY26 results, ASX 26 Aug 2026](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260826/pdf/0737tbnynjls6y.pdf)
- **Lynas implied cost (my calculation, not company-reported):** A$585.5m / 12,122 t REO is about A$48/kg REO against an average price of A$80.7/kg REO. This is blended across all products and uses accounting cost of sales (likely includes depreciation), so it is not an NdPr cash cost. — computed from the Lynas FY26 results above.
- **Lynas price context (verified):** Average China domestic NdPr price (ex-VAT) rose from US$55.0/kg in June 2025 to US$100.8/kg in June 2026. JARE agreement (March 2026): 12-year supply, firm 5,000 tpa NdPr, US$110/kg NdPr floor, upside sharing above US$150/kg capped at US$10m/yr. — [Lynas FY26 results](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260826/pdf/0737tbnynjls6y.pdf)
- **Lynas June 2026 quarter (search-summary only):** average selling price across all products a record A$98.2/kg; REO production 3,481 t. — [Lynas quarterly report, ASX 22 Jul 2026](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260722/pdf/071y3m2j4fp9px.pdf)
- **MP Materials (verified, Q2 2026 release, 6 Aug 2026):** NdPr production 840 t (+41% y/y), NdPr sales 1,006 t (+127%). NdPr oxide and metal revenue $94.4m; price protection agreement (PPA) income $17.6m; consolidated cost of sales ex-DD&A $72.3m (not split by product, includes magnetics). No NdPr cost per kg disclosed. Revenue / sales volume gives roughly $94/kg (my calculation; intercompany sales and oxide-equivalent conversion mean it is not the reported realized price). — [MP Materials Q2 2026 results](https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-reports-second-quarter-2026-results); [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001801368/000180136826000047/mpmcq22026er.htm)
- **MP realized price guidance (search-summary only):** management said Q3 realized NdPr oxide price should be in the "high $90s" per kg. — [Webull Q2 2026 call summary](https://www.webull.com/news/15398396334728192)
- **MP segment economics (search-summary only, analyst):** one analysis says Q2 Adjusted EBITDA was $28.5m, of which about $17.6m was PPA income, leaving a core of about $10.9m. Analyst estimate. — [Kavout](https://www.kavout.com/market-lens/mp-materials-beneath-the-ppa-fragile-profitability-meets-a-stretched-valuation)
- **Energy Fuels (search-summary only, company BFS press release dated about 15 Jan 2026):** Phase 2 circuit at White Mesa: capex about $410m, capacity above 6,000 tpa NdPr, estimated "all-in production cost of $29.39/kg NdPr", NPV8 $1.9bn, after-tax IRR 33%, commissioning late 2028-early 2029. Search summary attributes the cost to the Vara Mada (Madagascar) feedstock case, so it is feedstock-dependent. An earlier study gave capex $348m and processing cost $29.88/kg. I could not open the release (nasdaq.com returned 503). — [Energy Fuels press release via Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/energy-fuels-us-rare-earth-processing-expansion-boasts-lower-expected-capex); [Barchart copy](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/37056402/energy-fuels-u-s-rare-earth-processing-expansion-boasts-lower-than-expected-capex-significant-annual-ebitda-and-among-the-lowest-cost-ndpr-production-in-the-world)
- **Arafura Nolans (search-summary only; old studies):** Feb 2019 DFS operating cost US$25.94/kg NdPr oxide (net of phosphoric acid credit); May 2021 update headline US$24.76/kg with gross operating cost US$33.91/kg; later inventory update "less than US$24/kg" net of by-product credit. These are 2019-2021 study figures with study-date price/cost assumptions, not current. — [Arafura ASX announcement](https://wcsecure.weblink.com.au/pdf/ARU/02099546.pdf); [Stockhead](https://stockhead.com.au/resources/study-shows-arafura-has-a-long-life-low-cost-rare-earths-project-on-its-hands/); [Mining Weekly, 7 Feb 2019](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/arafura-releases-nolans-dfs-targets-construction-next-year-2019-02-07)
- **Iluka Eneabba (search-summary only):** no per-kg NdPr figure found. Only a dated 2022 illustration: about A$160m/yr cash operating cost on 12.4 ktpa REO output, roughly A$13/kg REO, excluding feedstock, sustaining capex and royalties. Capex: cumulative refinery spend A$1,101m at 30 Jun 2026, total estimate A$1.7-1.8bn. Capacity about 5.5 ktpa NdPr plus about 0.75 ktpa Dy/Tb, commissioning 2027. — [Iluka ASX Apr 2022](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20220404/pdf/457n00z958ghfd.pdf); [Iluka June 2026 quarterly](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260728/pdf/0723lrry1qs81t.pdf); [Iluka Dec 2024 presentation](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20241206/pdf/06cb7l8mtckl3w.pdf)
- **Incentive-price benchmarks for ex-China supply (search-summary only):** S&P Global says a structural cost range of US$75-80/kg NdPr is needed in 2028 to support about 27 kt of NdPr oxide across 20+ projects; Project Blue's estimate is US$75-105/kg. S&P June 2026 data: NdPr US$120/kg CIF North America vs US$110/kg FOB China. — [S&P Global NdPr market report, 8 May 2026 (PDF, could not be opened)](https://www.spglobal.com/content/dam/spglobal/global-assets/en/special-reports/critical-mineral-market-reports/Market%20Report%20-%20NdPr%20-%2008052026.pdf); [Cruxinvestor summary](https://www.cruxinvestor.com/posts/chinas-export-cap-shifts-rare-earth-margin-toward-producers)
- **Northern Rare Earth (search-summary only):** 2025 operating revenue 42.563bn yuan (+29.11%), net profit attributable 2.251bn yuan (+124.17%); annual report disclosed 18 Apr 2026. No cost per kg disclosed in what I saw. — [SMM](https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/103867901-china-northern-rare-earth-major-product-production-hit-record-highs-net-profit-in-2025-up-12417-yoy)

### Inferences
- Lynas's implied blended cost of about A$48/kg REO (roughly US$31/kg at about 0.65 USD/AUD, my conversion with an assumed FX rate) is in the same range as Energy Fuels' US$29/kg study figure and Arafura's US$24-34/kg. This suggests Western cash costs of roughly US$25-35/kg are plausible for light-REE-rich feeds, but accounting cost of sales is not a like-for-like cash cost. Incentive prices of US$75-105/kg reflect full capex recovery and risk, not operating cost.
- The Western "cost problem" is better framed as capital recovery plus ramp-up fixed cost (Lynas explicitly cites this) than as per-kg opex.

### Gaps
- No publicly reported all-in or opex cost per kg NdPr for MP, Lynas (unit-cost metric), or Iluka (current) was found.
- No cost per kg for Northern Rare Earth or Shenghe found in any accessible source; Chinese-vs-Western cost comparison is unavailable from public data in this search.
- Energy Fuels $29.39/kg not verified against the primary release, and the feedstock basis (Vara Mada vs other ore) is unresolved.
- Arafura's figures are 2019-2021; a newer 2025-2026 update was not found.

## 2. Finished NdFeB magnet price benchmarks outside China vs Chinese prices ($/kg), 2025-2026

### Takeaway
I found no public Benchmark, Fastmarkets, Argus or Adamas assessment of ex-China finished NdFeB magnet prices; those are paywalled. The only public figure is an undated Reuters-based claim of a US$10-30/kg premium for non-Chinese magnets. Chinese domestic magnet prices fell 1.2-1.8% Jan-Sep 2026 while NdPr oxide rose about 7%.

### Cited Findings
- **Premium claim (search-summary only, Reuters-based story, date not shown in results; likely 2025 after the April 2025 export controls, unverified):** a Neo Performance Materials executive said customers would pay US$10-30/kg above the usual Chinese magnet price; an EV uses 2-4 kg of magnets. NovaTech customers would pay 15-20% more for Vietnam-made magnets. The same story cites NdPr oxide at US$80/kg ex-China vs US$62 China (Fastmarkets). — [AOL/Reuters copy](https://www.aol.com/news/rare-earth-magnet-users-jolted-103239892.html); [AJOT copy](https://www.ajot.com/news/rare-earth-magnet-users-jolted-into-paying-premium-prices-for-ex-china-supply)
- **China domestic magnet vs oxide move (search-summary only, NextMSC Oct 2026, calculation on SMM data):** Pr-Nd oxide +7.0% from 15 Jan to 3 Sep 2026; four China-domestic NdFeB grades -1.2% to -1.8% over the same period. Secondary source. — [NextMSC](https://www.nextmsc.com/industry-deep-dive/rare-earth-price-shocks-magnet-cost)
- **Adamas (verified, Dec 2025 note):** ex-China NdFeB capacity pipeline for 2030 reached nearly 80,000 tpa as of Jan 2026, expected to approach or exceed 100,000 tpa in 2026. It says the MP-DoW price floor "offers a new benchmark for the ex-China industry" and expects similar floors elsewhere. Contains no magnet $/kg price. — [Adamas Top Predictions for 2026](https://www.adamasintel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Adamas-Intelligence-Rare-Earth-Magnet-Market-Outlook-Top-Predictions-for-2026.pdf)
- **Adamas Western CIF price forecast (Q2 2026 sample):** appeared in search results but was not opened. — [Adamas sample](https://www.adamasintel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Western-CIF-Price-Forecasts-Q2-2026-sample.pdf)
- **Fastmarkets (verified, 4 Dec 2023, outdated):** LCM chairman Grant Smith said there is "no way around" a premium for non-Chinese products. No premium figure given. Fastmarkets FOB China, 30 Nov 2023: NdPr oxide $68-70/kg, Dy oxide $370-380/kg, Tb oxide $1,100-1,140/kg. — [Fastmarkets](https://fastmarkets.com/insights/no-way-around-price-premium-for-non-chinese-rare-earth-products-less-common-metals-chairman)
- **MP magnetics (verified):** Magnetics segment Q2 2026 revenue $16.5m, segment adjusted EBITDA $7.5m; began NdFeB magnet manufacturing Dec 2025. No volume or price disclosed. — [MP Q2 2026 results](https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-reports-second-quarter-2026-results)

### Inferences
- A US$10-30/kg premium on a base Chinese magnet price (not located; historically tens of $/kg) implies a 20-50%-plus premium, but I cannot state the base price from sources found, so this ratio is not supported.
- Magnet price insensitivity to oxide in China (+7% vs -1.5%) suggests Chinese magnet makers are absorbing input costs, widening the ex-China gap.

### Gaps
- No Benchmark, Fastmarkets, Argus or Adamas public magnet $/kg price for ex-China or Chinese export, 2025-2026.
- No US OEM contract price anecdotes (Apple, GM, Vulcan, Noveon) found.
- Chinese export magnet price in $/kg not found (SMM pages returned no values in snippets).
- The Reuters premium story's date is unknown.

## 3. Supply-demand balance 2026-2035: NdPr and Dy/Tb (Benchmark, Adamas, Project Blue)

### Takeaway
Benchmark is publicly quoted as seeing an NdPr deficit through 2030; Adamas is quoted as seeing a heavy-rare-earth bottleneck outside China. No Project Blue or 2031-2035 balance figures were found publicly.

### Cited Findings
- **Benchmark (search-summary only; Engineering News, 2 Feb 2026):** NdPr market remains in deficit for the second consecutive year in 2026 and through the end of its forecast to 2030, as ex-China supply does not keep pace with demand. 2026 average NdPr oxide price forecast raised to US$90,000/t, with Q1 US$93,000/t and Q2 US$87,000/t. Article blocked (403) when I tried to open it. Actual Oct 2026 prices (US$100-120/kg) have exceeded this forecast. — [Engineering News](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/ndpr-oxide-price-forecast-to-rise-to-90-000t-this-year-report-2026-02-02); [Mining News](https://www.miningnews.net/energy-minerals/news/1424220/rare-earths-markets-remain-deficit-2030)
- **Benchmark on Dy (search-summary only, secondary write-up, unverified):** ex-China dysprosium could cost up to 8.3 times the Chinese domestic price by 2027, still about double by 2035. — see search result via [Skillings](https://www.skillings.net/?p=82100) (secondary; not confirmed against Benchmark)
- **Adamas (verified, Dec 2025):** China produces over 99% of refined Dy and Tb; availability outside China is a "near-term bottleneck" for Western magnet scale-up; heavy rare earth supply and metal-making capacity are expected bottlenecks for US magnets. No tonnage balance in the free note. — [Adamas Top Predictions for 2026](https://www.adamasintel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Adamas-Intelligence-Rare-Earth-Magnet-Market-Outlook-Top-Predictions-for-2026.pdf)
- **Adamas older forecast (search-summary only, date and methodology unknown, possibly outdated):** combined Nd/Pr/NdPr shortage rising to about 16,000 t in 2030. — [Adamas](https://www.adamasintel.com/unfathomable-rare-earth-demand-growth/)
- **Ex-China NdPr oxide supply vs capacity (search-summary only, S&P via Cruxinvestor):** announced ex-China NdPr oxide capacity could reach 58,000 t by 2028 but projected output only about 27,000 t. — [Cruxinvestor](https://www.cruxinvestor.com/posts/chinas-export-cap-shifts-rare-earth-margin-toward-producers)

### Inferences
- Benchmark and Adamas are directionally aligned (deficit/bottleneck), but the free quotes do not allow a year-by-year tonnage balance.

### Gaps
- No publicly quoted Project Blue figures found (only a Project Blue price estimate of US$75-105/kg cited secondhand via S&P).
- No 2031-2035 NdPr or Dy/Tb balance numbers (tonnes) found from any of the three.
- No Dy/Tb 2026-2030 deficit tonnage found.
