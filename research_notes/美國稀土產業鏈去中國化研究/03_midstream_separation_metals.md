# US Midstream Rare Earth Supply Chain: Separation into Oxides and Reduction into Metals/Alloys (as of 22 Oct 2026)

Scope notes: "Operating" means a plant that produces commercial product today. "Commissioning" means it is built or mechanically complete and starting feed. "Planned" means announced, in engineering, or under construction. Allied (non-US) facilities that explicitly serve US buyers are flagged **[ALLIED]**. MP Materials' Q3 2026 results were not yet published at the time of research (Q2 2026 results came out 6 Aug 2026). Source-hygiene note: the Motley Fool transcript page for MP's Q2 2026 call contained embedded instructions aimed at AI tools. They were ignored, and only the factual call content was used.

## 1. MP Materials: Mountain Pass separation, heavy REE circuit, Independence metal/magnets

### Takeaway
Mountain Pass is the only US light rare earth (NdPr) separation operation at real commercial scale. It produced 2,599 t of NdPr oxide in 2025 and is running at about 3.5–4 kt/yr in 2026 (Q3 2026 guided above 1,000 t). It is still well below the plant's design rate, and "mid-stream reliability issues" are ongoing. The DoD-backed heavy REE (Dy/Tb) circuit reached mechanical completion in May 2026, but no Dy/Tb output had been reported as of the Q2 call. Independence (Fort Worth) began NdFeB magnet manufacturing in Dec 2025, and commercial GM shipments are expected in Q4 2026.

### Inferences
- MP's 2026 NdPr output looks likely to land around 3.6–3.9 kt: Q1 917 + Q2 840 + Q3 >1,000 + Q4 roughly 1,000. That is about 40–50% above 2025, but still short of the multi-kt design rate the company has long targeted. Management itself flags mid-stream reliability.
- MP's commercial Dy/Tb volumes in calendar 2026 will likely be small: first product only, at a pace limited by quality. Mountain Pass bastnäsite is very low in heavies, so meaningful Dy/Tb tonnage depends on outside feed (recycled material or third-party concentrates).
- The roughly 650 t of oxide/metal inventory and the gap between sales and production show that metallization lead time is a real constraint inside MP's own chain.

### Gaps
- MP has not disclosed the nameplate capacity of the heavy REE circuit (t/yr of Dy/Tb), its feed source, or the date of first Dy/Tb output. Q3 2026 results (likely early Nov 2026) had not been published.
- Individual 2024 Q1–Q3 NdPr figures were not retrieved; only the full year (1,294 t) and Q4 (413 t).
- No disclosed NdPr metal capacity at Independence, and no confirmed 10X nameplate from a primary source.
- MP does not disclose NdPr cost per kg.

## 2. Energy Fuels: White Mesa Mill (Utah)

### Takeaway
White Mesa has installed Phase 1 NdPr separation capacity of about 1,000 tpa (1,049 tpa), but runs it in feed-limited campaigns, and 2025 tonnage was not disclosed. Dy and Tb oxide exist only at pilot scale: about 29 kg of Dy by Dec 2025, and Tb at about 1 kg/week from March 2026. Commercial Dy/Tb slipped from end-2026 to 2027. Construction of the heavy REE plant began 29 Jul 2026, with Tb/Dy circuits due by end-2027. Phase 2 (total about 6,562 tpa NdPr, 200 tpa Dy, 60 tpa Tb) awaits licensing (mid-2027) and FID, with commissioning targeted around Q1 2029. Energy Fuels is also buying VAC (magnets, including Sumter SC) and ASM (Korean metals plant plus Dubbo) to move into metals and magnets.

### Cited Findings
- **Phase 1:**
  - existing NdPr recovery 1,049 tpa
  - planned heavies 35 tpa Dy and 12 tpa Tb, plus Sm/Eu/Gd and possibly Y/Lu
  - operational in 2027, subject to approvals, financing and monazite feed of up to about 10,000 t/yr (yielding 850–1,000 t NdPr)
  - commercial Dy/Tb previously targeted for end-2026, now moved to 2027
  — [Energy Fuels 2025 results and 2026 guidance, 26 Feb 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-02-26-Energy-Fuels-Announces-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance)
- **Conflicting heavy REE sizing:** Crux Investor reports the expansion is designed for about 20 tpa Tb oxide and 120 tpa Dy oxide. That conflicts with the company's 35 tpa Dy and 12 tpa Tb for Phase 1 — [Crux Investor](https://www.cruxinvestor.com/posts/energy-fuels-white-mesa-mill-produces-first-us-terbium-oxide-in-decades-targets-commercial-heavy-rare-earth-output-by-2027) vs [EF 26 Feb 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-02-26-Energy-Fuels-Announces-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance)
- **Dy pilot:**
  - first kg of 99.9% Dy oxide in Aug 2025
  - about 29 kg of high-purity Dy oxide by Dec 2025
  - qualified by a Korean magnet maker, and by a Japanese manufacturer for DyFe production
  — [EF, 21 Aug 2025](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2025-08-21-Energy-Fuels-Successfully-Produces-First-Kilogram-of-99-9-Purity-Dysprosium-Oxide-at-its-White-Mesa-Mill-in-Utah-on-Track-to-Commence-Terbium-Production-in-Q4-2025); [EF, 19 Dec 2025](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2025-12-19-Energy-Fuels-US-Produced-Heavy-Rare-Earth-Oxide-Successfully-Qualified-for-Use-in-Permanent-Magnets)
- **Tb pilot:** first kg of 99.9% Tb oxide in about March 2026, produced at roughly 1 kg/week — [Metal Tech News, 1 Apr 2026](https://www.metaltechnews.com/story/2026/04/01/tech-metals/energy-fuels-produces-terbium-at-white-mesa/2698.html); [Crux Investor](https://www.cruxinvestor.com/posts/energy-fuels-white-mesa-mill-produces-first-us-terbium-oxide-in-decades-targets-commercial-heavy-rare-earth-output-by-2027)
- **Heavy REE plant construction:**
  - construction began (announced 29 Jul 2026)
  - Tb/Dy circuits to be complete by end-2027, Sm/Eu/Gd by end-2028
  - an MREC circuit is planned so REE and uranium can be produced at the same time
  - sized for near-term monazite from the Donald JV
  - the Q2 release gives no capacity figures
  — [EF Q2 2026 results, 5 Aug 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-08-05-Energy-Fuels-Announces-Q2-2026-Results)
- **Phase 2:**
  - standalone capacity (40-year average): 5,513 tpa NdPr, 48 tpa Tb, 165 tpa Dy
  - combined with Phase 1: about 6,562 tpa NdPr, 60 tpa Tb, 200 tpa Dy
  - capex $410M; NPV(8%) $1.9B; IRR 33%
  - no FID yet
  — [EF 26 Feb 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-02-26-Energy-Fuels-Announces-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance)
- **Phase 2 licensing and timing:** regulatory approval expected mid-2027; construction and commissioning targeted for Q1 2029 — [EF Q2 2026 via Nasdaq/search](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/energy-fuels-announces-q2-2026-results-2026-08-05)
- **Phase 2 filings:** the license amendment application was filed Nov 2025 and the Phase 2 feasibility study in early Jan 2026 — [EF 2026 DEF 14A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1385849/000106299326002046/formdef14a.htm)
- **Conflicting Phase 2 figures:** one secondary report gives 80 t Tb, 288 t Dy, and a 2029 target — [search summary of 10-K/secondary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1385849/000138584926000009/efr-20251231.htm)
- **Financing and M&A:**
  - conditional $725M OSC loan commitment
  - VAC acquisition (definitive agreement 23 Jun 2026, about $1.9B cash and stock). VAC's Sumter, SC magnet plant has 2,000 tpa capacity, scalable to 12,000 tpa
  - ASM acquisition (Dubbo, plus a metallization/alloying facility in South Korea); Australian FIRB approval obtained; close expected end-Aug 2026
  — [EF Q2 2026 results](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-08-05-Energy-Fuels-Announces-Q2-2026-Results)
- **Feed:**
  - Donald JV (Australia, with Astron): EF stake 12.7%, can earn up to 49%, holds 100% monazite offtake rights, FID possible as early as Q3 2026
  - Vara Mada (Madagascar): all-in cost $29.39/kg NdPr-equivalent, FID subject to Malagasy fiscal and permitting terms
  — [EF Q2 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-08-05-Energy-Fuels-Announces-Q2-2026-Results); [EF 26 Feb 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-02-26-Energy-Fuels-Announces-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance)
- **Offtake:** an Aug 2025 MOU to supply NdPr and Dy oxide to Vulcan Elements for validation — [EF 26 Feb 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-02-26-Energy-Fuels-Announces-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance)

### Inferences
- White Mesa is the only US site licensed to handle monazite's uranium/thorium content at scale. That licensing moat is a strategic advantage. The weak points are feed (Donald and Vara Mada are not yet producing) and the timing of license amendments.
- Realistic commercial US Dy/Tb from Energy Fuels: about 47 tpa combined from 2028, and about 260 tpa only after Phase 2, around 2029–2030, if FID, licensing and feed all land.
- The ASM and VAC deals would make Energy Fuels a vertically integrated oxide-to-metal-to-magnet group. The metal step, however, sits in Korea [ALLIED], not the US.

### Gaps
- Actual 2025 and 2026 YTD NdPr oxide tonnage was not disclosed in the releases reviewed.
- Whether the ASM deal had closed as of Oct 2026 was not confirmed.
- No capacity of the Korean metals plant was retrieved in this session.

## 3. Lynas: Seadrift, Texas heavy REE plant and Lynas Malaysia heavy REE output

### Takeaway
Lynas's Seadrift, Texas plant has not been built. As of 2026 it is effectively stalled or uncertain: Lynas warned in Aug 2025 that it "may not proceed" without an acceptable DoD offtake, its original July 2025–June 2026 operating window has passed, and no construction start was found. Lynas Malaysia [ALLIED] is the only commercial-scale ex-China Dy/Tb separator: 8 t in the March 2026 quarter and 19 t in the June 2026 quarter. Some of that supply flows to the US through a DoW oxide-purchase LOI.

### Cited Findings
- Aug 2025: Lynas said that without an acceptable offtake agreement with the US DoD, Seadrift "may not proceed" — [CNBC, 28 Aug 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/28/lynas-flags-uncertainty-over-texas-rare-earths-plant-posts-profit-slump.html); [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/web/australias-lynas-posts-over-90-fall-in-annual-profit-to-raise-487-million/)
- The original design was about 2,500–3,000 tpa of heavy REE products, with operations targeted for July 2025–June 2026. A 2026 directory profile says "significant uncertainty" remains. In March 2026, Lynas signed a binding LOI with the Department of War for about $96M of oxide purchases over 4 years — [Rare Earth Directory (2026)](https://rareearthdirectory.com/rare-earth-companies-in-the-us/lynas-usa/)
- An April 2026 report says Lynas is "in negotiations" to build Seadrift and had secured $258M from the DoW — [TIME, 20 Apr 2026](https://time.com/article/2026/04/20/trump-s-push-to-break-china-s-dominance-of-critical-rare-earth-minerals/)
- Lynas Malaysia [ALLIED] first produced Dy and Tb oxide in May–June 2025 — [Fastmarkets](https://fastmarkets.com/insights/lynas-to-produce-dysprosium-terbium-oxide-in-malaysia)
- March 2026 quarter: 8 t of Dy+Tb, plus first samarium oxide. June 2026 quarter: 3,481 t TREO, 1,857 t NdPr, 19 t Dy+Tb (described as first commercial production) — [Kalkine Media](https://kalkinemedia.com/au/news/announcements/lynas-rare-earths-reports-record-a2889-million-revenue-in-june-2026-quarter-amid-heavy-rare-earth-expansion); [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104021087-lynas-reports-8-increase-in-rare-earth-production-despite-pr-nd-decline-sales-revenue-up-70-yoy)
- The Malaysia heavy REE expansion cost rose from about A$180M to about A$294M. Original design was about 1,500 tpa of mixed SEG+heavy (SEGH) feed — [Kalkine/Fastmarkets via search](https://fastmarkets.com/insights/lynas-to-produce-dysprosium-terbium-oxide-in-malaysia)

### Inferences
- Lynas Malaysia's run rate is about 76 t/yr of Dy+Tb (19 t × 4). That likely exceeds all US Dy/Tb oxide production through 2027, so in practice the US heavy REE supply chain depends on this allied source.
- Seadrift should be treated as "announced / not under construction." It should not be counted in US 2026–2028 capacity.

### Gaps
- No 2026 primary Lynas statement confirming Seadrift cancellation or FID was found.
- The tonnage split of Lynas heavy oxide going to US buyers was not found.

## 4. Other separation players: Ucore, ReElement, Aclara, Phoenix Tailings, USA Rare Earth, Vulcan, Solvay/Syensqo, Neo, Noveon, others

- Through 2027, US light REE separation is effectively a duopoly of MP plus Energy Fuels. New entrants (Ucore, ReElement) would add hundreds of tonnes of TREO at most before 2027–2028.
- The first new heavy-REE-dedicated US plants are Energy Fuels (end-2027) and Aclara (2028+, feed-dependent).

### Gaps
- No verified current tonnages for ReElement or Ucore. Ucore's H2 2026 commissioning was not confirmed as of Oct 2026.

- Noveon's own oxide/metal throughput is not disclosed.

## 5. Metal and alloy making (NdPr metal, DyFe, TbFe): who can do it in the US, and capacity in tpa

### Takeaway
Oxide-to-metal conversion is now widely seen as the tightest US bottleneck, because separation is expanding faster than metallization. US-located metal capacity in 2026 is in the low hundreds of tonnes outside MP's in-house (undisclosed) metallization: Phoenix Tailings Exeter, NH starts at 200 tpa, including DyFe. The largest Western metal/alloy maker serving the US is LCM in the UK [ALLIED, now owned by USA Rare Earth], at 2,500 tpa going to 3,000 tpa by Q4 2026.

Rare Earth Exchanges' own view is that "genuinely resilient" mine-to-magnet capacity is closer to 20% around 2030–31 — [Rare Earth Exchanges, 10 Aug 2026](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/u-s-rare-earth-projects-could-supply-86-of-2030-ndpr-oxide-demand-but-the-supply-chain-tells-a-different-story/)
- **Phoenix Tailings (Exeter, NH; metal facility opened Oct 2025):**

  — [Business Wire, 23 Oct 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251023800456/en/Phoenix-Tailings-Opens-Cutting-Edge-U.S.-Rare-Earth-Metallization-Facility-Entirely-Independent-From-China); [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/metals-platform/newsandanalysis/article/2685053-Phoenix-Tailings-raises--76mn-for-US-REE-plant); [Business Wire, 30 Apr 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250430735778/en/Rare-Earth-Metals-Refining-Company-Phoenix-Tailings-Closes-on-Additional-$33M-in-Funding-From-Strategic-Investors-Bringing-the-Round-up-to-$76M-to-Support-Rare-Earth-Metal-Independence)

## 6. Process technology risks: SX know-how, reagents, radioactive waste/licensing, ramp-up history (Molycorp)

### Takeaway
Ramp-up risk is the dominant midstream risk, and it is visible today. MP still reports reliability issues in mid-stream circuits more than five years after restarting separation. Energy Fuels' commercial heavy REE slipped about a year. Lynas Malaysia's heavy REE expansion went about 63% over budget. Lynas Texas stalled. Molycorp's Project Phoenix is the historic warning. Radioactive-material licensing (thorium/uranium in monazite) is a gating item that favors the already-licensed White Mesa site.

### Cited Findings
- MP Q2 2026: Mountain Pass is "still working through reliability issues in a handful of mid-stream circuits," which hurt yield and throughput. There was a planned semi-annual shutdown in April 2026, and chlor-alkali (in-house reagent) recommissioning is in progress — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/mp-materials-mp-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Energy Fuels moved commercial Dy/Tb from end-2026 to 2027. Expansions are "subject to regulatory approvals, financing and monazite feedstock" — [EF 26 Feb 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-02-26-Energy-Fuels-Announces-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance)
- Phase 2 needs a radioactive materials license amendment, filed Nov 2025, with approval expected mid-2027 — [EF DEF 14A 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1385849/000106299326002046/formdef14a.htm); [EF Q2 2026 via Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/energy-fuels-announces-q2-2026-results-2026-08-05)
- White Mesa is "the only fully licensed conventional uranium mill operating in the U.S." — [EF Q2 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-08-05-Energy-Fuels-Announces-Q2-2026-Results)
- Lynas Malaysia's heavy REE expansion rose from about A$180M to A$294M because of equipment costs and geopolitics. The Lynas CEO said new plant development "can be long and uncertain" — [Kalkine/Fastmarkets via search](https://fastmarkets.com/insights/lynas-to-produce-dysprosium-terbium-oxide-in-malaysia); [CNBC, 28 Aug 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/28/lynas-flags-uncertainty-over-texas-rare-earths-plant-posts-profit-slump.html)
- **Molycorp (Project Phoenix, 2008–2015):**
  - about $419.9M spent on Phases 1–2 by end-2011, against an $895M total budget
  - in 2012 the separations and finishing facilities were still under construction
  - risk factors flagged funding gaps, delays, cost overruns and weak offtake
  - commentators cite immature separation technology and Chinese price manipulation as causes of the collapse
  — [Molycorp 10-K FY2011](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001489137/000104746912001655/a2207459z10-k.htm); [Molycorp 424B5 2012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001489137/000119312512363495/d396920d424b5.htm); [Defense News, Nov 2019](https://www.defensenews.com/opinion/commentary/2019/11/12/the-collapse-of-american-rare-earth-mining-and-lessons-learned/); [Jewish Policy Center, Oct 2020](https://www.jewishpolicycenter.org/2020/10/02/lessons-from-the-collapse-of-u-s-rare-earths-production/)
- The NLR/DOE review notes that new plants rarely reach full capacity within one to two years of commissioning — [Rare Earth Exchanges](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/u-s-rare-earth-projects-could-supply-86-of-2030-ndpr-oxide-demand-but-the-supply-chain-tells-a-different-story/)
- Ucore's 2026 milestones are explicitly conditional on construction, commissioning, qualification, funding and permits — [Ucore](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/308111/Ucore-Verifies-Louisiana-RapidSXTM-Equipment-ScaleUp-Configurations)

### Inferences
- New, unproven separation technologies (RapidSX, chromatography) carry scale-up risk. Conventional SX carries know-how and labor risk: heavy REE SX needs hundreds of mixer-settler stages and long equilibration, and the Western engineering bench outside Lynas, Solvay and MP is thin. Planning should assume ramps of 2–3 years after commissioning.
- Price floors and offtakes (MP's $110/kg; DoW purchases from Lynas) directly address Molycorp's failure mode, which was price collapse without guaranteed offtake. Technical ramp risk remains unhedged.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data found on reagent (HCl, NaOH, extractants such as P507/D2EHPA) supply dependence on China, or on US environmental compliance cost per kg.
- NRC versus Agreement-State (Utah DEQ) jurisdiction details were not retrieved.

## 7. Cost comparison vs Chinese separators (opex per kg) and environmental costs
