## Q1. Mountain Pass (MP Materials, CA): output, reserves, grade, expansion, heavy REE

### Takeaway
Mountain Pass is the only US primary rare earth mine at scale. In 2025 it produced a record 50,692 t of REO in concentrate, about 13% of world mine output. It has about 28 years of reserves at 5.89% REO. All concentrate sales (formerly to China) stopped in July 2025, and the full stream now goes to MP's own separation plant (NdPr oxide 2,599 t in 2025; Q3 2026 guided above 1,000 t). The ore is bastnaesite, and its heavy-REE content is low and not quantified in public filings. MP's first Dy/Tb circuit reached mechanical completion in May 2026 and targets first Tb/Dy "later this year" (2026). MP says it can take third-party heavy feed.

### Cited Findings
**Production history (REO in concentrate, metric tons)**
- 2021: 42,400 t; 2022: 42,500 t; 2023: 41,600 t; 2024: 45,500 t (reported); 2025e: 51,000 t (USGS, US total = essentially Mountain Pass bastnaesite) — [USGS MCS 2026, Rare Earths](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf) (Feb 2026)
- FY2025: record 50,692 t REO, up 12% YoY. Q4 2025: 12,080 t REO. FY2025 NdPr oxide production 2,599 t ("roughly double" 2024), NdPr sales a record 1,994 t — [MP Q4/FY2025 results](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/MP-Materials-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx); [MP press release](https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results/) (Feb 2026)
- Q4 2025 NdPr production 718 t. MP exited 2025 at an annualized NdPr run rate of about 4,000 t — [MP Materials on X](https://x.com/MPMaterials/status/2027128896022511879) (Feb 2026)
- Q1 2026: record 12,983 t REO, +6% YoY — [Yahoo Finance/Zacks](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mp-kicks-off-2026-record-172500564.html) (May 2026)
- Q2 2026: 11,072 t REO, −16% YoY vs 13,145 t in Q2 2025, because of the scheduled semi-annual outage and pilot testing. NdPr oxide production 840 t (+41% vs 597 t). NdPr sales 1,006 t (+127%). Concentrate revenue $0, since "concentrate sales ceased beginning July 2025" — [MP Q2 2026 results](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/MP-Materials-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx) (Aug 2026)
- Management: "I expect Q3 NdPr production to exceed 1,000 metric tons." About 650 t of NdPr oxide/metal was on hand or in transit at June 30, 2026 — [MP Q2 2026 call transcript, Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/mp-materials-mp-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) (Aug 13, 2026)
- 2026 YTD REO (Q1+Q2) = 24,055 t (computed from the figures above).

**Reserves, grade, mine life**
- Proven + probable reserves (SRK, S-K 1300): about 28.96 million short tons of ore at 5.89% REO, about 1.96 million short tons of contained REO, 2.50% TREO cut-off. Mine life about 28 years. The prior year was about 29.69 Mst at 5.97%, 2.04 Mst REO, 29 years. The TRS (Exhibit 96.1) is dated effective Oct 1, 2025 and reported Feb 16, 2026 — [MP FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136826000008/mp-20251231.htm); [Exhibit 96.1](https://www.boardroomalpha.com/sec/mp-10-k-2026-02-26-0001801368-26-000008/mpmcexhibit961123125.htm)
- MP uses dry-stack tailings, which avoid wet tailings ponds and dams. Tailings/dewatering supplies about 95% of beneficiation water. "Storage and handling of naturally occurring radioactive material" appears among the regulatory requirements — [MP FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136826000008/mp-20251231.htm)

**Expansion ("Upstream 60K")**
- Announced Nov 2023: lift annual REO to about 60,000 t by further beneficiation and use of lower-grade ore. The FY2024 10-Q aimed to reach this "within the next four years with modest incremental capital" — [MP 10-K FY2025 / 10-Q 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136826000008/mp-20251231.htm)
- Q2 2026 call: exploratory drilling suggests "the potential for additional ore within the existing pit contours." A new pre-flotation process is expected at scale "by 2028." Chlor-alkali benefits "build progressively through '27." The call gave no REO capacity guidance — [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/mp-materials-mp-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

Unverified against filings, and it conflicts with the reported quarters, which peak around 13,145–13,250 t — [Quartr summary](https://quartr.com/events/mp-materials-corp-mp-canaccord-genuity-s-46th-annual-growth-conference_3sNK7nzf)

**Heavy REE at Mountain Pass**
- Aug 2025: the DoW (Department of War, formerly DoD) gave MP a $150 million direct loan to build a heavy-REE separation facility at Mountain Pass — [USGS MCS 2026, Rare Earths (Heavy)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths-heavy.pdf)
- The HREE facility will "primarily produce terbium and dysprosium," and MP sells SEG+ (mixed heavy) concentrate. The 10-K expects commissioning in 2026 and gives no tpa capacity — [MP FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136826000008/mp-20251231.htm)
- Mechanical completion of the first heavy-REE circuit came in May 2026. "We are preparing to introduce feed to the circuit imminently ... on track to produce terbium and dysprosium later this year." The circuit "has the ability to process third-party feedstocks." Samarium first production is planned for 2028. A gadolinium long-term offtake with a US aerospace/defense manufacturer is "a sizable 9-figure deal" — [Q2 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/mp-materials-mp-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Timeline drift: the Q3 2025 release targeted "mid-2026" heavy-REE commissioning ([8-K Q3 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136825000052/mpmcq32025er.htm)), while coverage of the Q4 2025 call said separated heavies by late 2026 ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mp-materials-corp-mp-q4-050141260.html))

**Downstream context (affects how much feedstock is consumed domestically)**
- 10X magnet facility: commissioning expected 2028, about 7,000 t/yr magnets. With Independence (TX) at 3,000 t/yr, the total is about 10,000 t/yr. DoW guarantees at least $140M EBITDA. Apple long-term supply deal signed July 2025 — [MP FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136826000008/mp-20251231.htm). "10X is 100% contracted with the Department of War" — [Q2 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/mp-materials-mp-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

### Inferences
- Q1 2025 REO was about 12,250 t (from Q1 2026's +6% YoY), and Q3 2025 is about 13,200 t by subtraction from FY2025 = 50,692 t. These are derived figures, not reported ones.
- The NdPr content of about 50,000 t REO is roughly 15–16% for bastnaesite. That implies about 7,500–8,000 t NdPr oxide contained per year. Actual separated NdPr (2,599 t in 2025; run rate of about 4,000 t exiting 2025; Q3 2026 above 1,000 t) means separation recovery and throughput, not mining, is the binding constraint. The 15–16% share is general industry knowledge for Mountain Pass bastnaesite and was not sourced here, so the writer should treat it as approximate.
- If Upstream 60K is reached (around 2027–28), US mine output would be about 60 kt REO, still a light-REE stream. Mountain Pass's Dy/Tb output from its own ore will be small, so the HREE circuit's value depends on imported or third-party heavy feed.

### Gaps
- No MP Q3 2026 figures (not yet released or not found).
- No public quantification of Dy/Tb grade in Mountain Pass ore, and no nameplate capacity (tpa) for the HREE circuit.
- 2024 NdPr production (believed to be about 1,294 t) was not verified in a primary source this session.
- Saudi Maaden JV status was not covered in the sources fetched.

## Q2. Development projects: project-by-project database

### Takeaway
Apart from Mountain Pass, no US primary REE mine is producing, and none has a full feasibility study plus all permits plus financing. The most advanced are Elk Creek (NE; FS Aug 2026, about $1.85B capex, EXIM targeting about 65%, but REEs are a by-product) and Bear Lodge (WY; FAST-41 covered March 2026, permitting target March–mid 2028, mine start target 2030). Halleck Creek (WY) and Round Top (TX) are at the PEA/PFS stage. Brook Mine (WY, coal-hosted) has only an inferred resource and a PEA. The realistic earliest new US mine output is 2028–2030. Energy Fuels' feed strategy relies mostly on foreign monazite (Australia, Madagascar, Brazil) processed at White Mesa, UT.

## Q3. Total US mine output vs US consumption; share of global production (USGS MCS 2025/2026)

### Takeaway
The US mined about 51,000 t REO in 2025 (45,500 t in 2024), about 13% of world output (390,000 t), second to China (270,000 t). It holds only about 1.9 Mt of the world's more than 75 Mt reserves (about 2.5%). The US is a net exporter of concentrate but still imports most compounds and metals. Net import reliance for compounds/metals was 67% in 2025 and 100% for heavy REEs, and terbium and holmium imports came 100% from China (2021–24).

### Cited Findings
- **MCS 2026 (Feb 2026):** US mineral-concentrate production 2021–2025e: 42,400; 42,500; 41,600; 45,500; 51,000 t REO. The 2025 value was $240M. Compounds/metals production: 120; 95; 800; 4,300; 8,900 t. Imports of compounds: 7,730; 10,800; 8,970; 8,120; 21,000 t (imports +169% in 2025). Exports of ores/compounds: 45,700; 45,900; 20,700; 37,500; 14,000 t. **Apparent consumption (compounds and metals): 7,900; 10,200; 8,600; 9,010; 27,000 t.** Net import reliance (compounds/metals): >95%, >95%, >90%, 53%, 67%. Concentrates: net exporter. Mine/mill employment: 670 (2025). The leading domestic end use is catalysts; the global leading use is magnets — [USGS MCS 2026 Rare Earths](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)
- **World mine production 2024 / 2025e (t REO):** US 45,500 / 51,000; Australia 29,000 / 29,000; Brazil 560 / 2,000; Burma 27,000 / 22,000; China 270,000 / 270,000; India 2,900 / 2,900; Madagascar 1,400 / 2,700; Malaysia 140 / 110; Nigeria 1,500 / 1,500; Russia 2,600 / 2,600; Thailand 2,100 / 4,800; Vietnam 300 / 150; World 380,000 / 390,000 — [USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)

North American M&I resources: 3.6 Mt in the US and more than 14 Mt in Canada — [USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)
- USGS concentrate figures exclude monazite concentrates (southeastern US mineral sands) — [USGS MCS 2026, footnote 2](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)
- **MCS 2025 (Jan 2025, 2024e data):** about 45,000 t REO in concentrates valued at $260M. Apparent consumption of compounds/metals in 2024e was 6,600 t, later revised to 9,010 in MCS 2026. Exports of ores/compounds were 43,000 t in 2024e, later revised to 37,500 — [USGS MCS 2025 Rare Earths](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-rare-earths.pdf)
- Import sources for compounds/metals (2021–24): China 71%, Malaysia 13%, Japan 5%, Estonia 5%, other 6% — [USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)
- Heavy REE: net import reliance 100% in every year 2021–2025. Tb and Ho from China 100%, Lu from China 100%. Imports of heavy compounds/metals were about 100 t in 2025e. Prices (2025e, $/kg): Tb oxide 1,010; Dy oxide 239 — [USGS MCS 2026 Rare Earths (Heavy)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths-heavy.pdf)
- NdPr oxide price: $55/kg (2024) and $69/kg (2025e) — [USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)
- FY2025 National Defense Stockpile potential acquisitions included 300 t NdPr oxide, 450 t NdFeB magnet block and 60 t SmCo alloy — [USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)

### Inferences
- US share of world mine output: 45,500/380,000 ≈ 12.0% (2024) and 51,000/390,000 ≈ 13.1% (2025). US share of world reserves is about 2.5%.
- USGS apparent consumption (27,000 t in 2025) covers only compounds and metals. It excludes the very large REE content embedded in imported magnets, motors and electronics, so true US demand is understated. US mine output (51 kt REO) exceeds apparent consumption, but this mismatch is in form: the US mined concentrate and exported most of it to China until mid-2025, while importing separated products and magnets.
- Since July 2025, MP's concentrate is retained domestically. That is why 2025 exports fell (37,500 → 14,000 t) and domestic compound production rose (4,300 → 8,900 t).

### Gaps
- No authoritative estimate was found for total US REE demand including embedded magnets, or for US NdPr/Dy/Tb demand in tonnes. Other researchers covering magnets/demand may have this.

## Q4. Structural light/heavy imbalance; domestic heavy REE sources; Th/U handling

### Takeaway
US mine supply is light-REE (bastnaesite at Mountain Pass). No US mine produced commercial Dy/Tb in 2025 (USGS). Identified domestic heavy-REE options are small or distant: Elk Creek (67 t/yr Dy oxide, 17 t/yr Tb oxide, after construction), Round Top (about 72% HREE+Y but about 0.06% grade), Bokan-Dotson (HREE-rich, long-term), Brook Mine (unproven), Lemhi/Idaho belt (exploration), and US mineral-sand monazite. In the near term, the US separation plants (MP, Energy Fuels, Ucore) will rely mostly on foreign heavy feed: Australian monazite (Donald, 0.37% Tb and 2.15% Dy of TREO), Madagascar, Brazilian ionic clays and recycling. Thorium/uranium in monazite and carbonatite ores pushes processing toward NRC/Agreement-State-licensed sites (e.g., White Mesa).

### Cited Findings
- "Minerals containing heavy rare earths were mined domestically in 2025 ... several produced small-scale quantities of heavy-rare-earth compounds and metals in 2025, although none produced sustained commercial-scale quantities." At least five companies were developing commercial-scale heavy-REE processing — [USGS MCS 2026 Heavy](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths-heavy.pdf)
- China export controls: April 2025 added Sm, Gd, Tb, Dy, Lu, Sc, Y. October 2025 extended them to Eu, Ho, Er, Tm, Yb, but the October measures were suspended for 1 year in November 2025, while the April controls remained with general licenses issued to select exporters — [USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf)
- Federal heavy-REE support: $150M DoW loan to MP (Aug 2025). $80M DoW loan to a recycler in Marion, IN and $5.1M to a Houston recycler for Tb/Dy recovery. DFC approved a $465M loan (Nov 2025) to a Brazilian ionic-clay producer to boost heavy-REE output — [USGS MCS 2026 Heavy](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths-heavy.pdf)
- Domestic heavy-REE volumes planned: Elk Creek 67 t/yr Dy oxide and 17 t/yr Tb oxide — [NioCorp FS webcast](https://www.niocorp.com/wp-content/uploads/08-11-2026_NioCorp_FSResults_Webcast_vFINAL.pdf). Energy Fuels Phase 1 at 35 t Dy and 12 t Tb in 2027, Phase 1+2 at about 200 t Dy and 60 t Tb, both mostly from imported monazite — [EF Feb 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-02-26-Energy-Fuels-Announces-2025-Results-and-2026-Guidance). Round Top is about 72% HREO+Y — [TMRC fact sheet](https://www.tmrcorp.com/_resources/Fact_Sheet.pdf). Bear Lodge "also holds Heavy REEs: Dy & Tb," with future HREE separation possible — [RER presentation](https://rareelementresources.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Corporate-Presentation.pdf)
- Ucore Louisiana SMC: under construction, initially Tb/Dy, with Bokan as a later domestic HREE feed — [Ucore Dec 2025](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/ucore-readies-louisiana-2026-heavy-rare-earth-element-processing-2025-12-11)
- **Th/U regulation:** 10 CFR 40.13(c) exempts rare earth metals/compounds with ≤0.25% by weight Th+U from licensing, per a historic NRC docket (Molycorp, 1982) — [NRC ML2006 doc](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2006/ML20063A813.pdf). An NRC record shows monazite plants with feeds/products below 500 ppm source material needing no license, while full-scale monazite production required one — [NRC docket](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2006/ML20063A856.pdf). Residuals from monazite processing for thorium recovery were deemed 11e.(2) byproduct material (2002 NRC letter) — [NRC ML0203](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML0203/ML020370263.pdf). Heritage Minerals (NJ) case on monazite-rich product — [NRC TAR](https://www.nrc.gov/facilities-safety/radiation-protection/technical-assistance-request-heritage-minerals-inc-possession-and-transfer-of-monazite-rich-product)
- White Mesa (UT) is the only fully licensed conventional uranium mill operating in the US, which is why it can process monazite and recover U from it — [EF Q2 2026](https://investors.energyfuels.com/2026-08-05-Energy-Fuels-Announces-Q2-2026-Results)
- Sheep Creek opposition explicitly cites radioactive thorium in material to be trucked — [Montana Free Press](https://montanafreepress.org/2026/07/27/forest-service-reviews-exploratory-mining-projects-in-the-headwaters-of-two-montana-rivers/). RER's flowsheet advertises "early Th" removal and zero discharge — [RER presentation](https://rareelementresources.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Corporate-Presentation.pdf). MP lists NORM storage/handling among its regulatory requirements — [MP 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136826000008/mp-20251231.htm)

### Inferences
- Even with every announced US project on schedule, domestic primary Dy/Tb supply before 2030 is in the tens of tonnes per year: Elk Creek about 67 t Dy and 17 t Tb, and Energy Fuels' 35 t Dy and 12 t Tb mostly from foreign monazite. Separation capacity for Dy/Tb (MP, EF, Ucore) is likely to arrive before domestic heavy feed does, so heavy feed will be imported (Australia, Brazil, Madagascar, Malaysia/Lynas) or recycled.
- Monazite-based routes (Energy Fuels, the Idaho belt, US mineral sands) carry the most Th burden. Bastnaesite and carbonatite routes (MP, Bear Lodge) produce less, but they still generate NORM residues requiring state-licensed disposal.

## Q5. Discovery-to-production timelines and permitting durations in the US

### Takeaway
The US averages about 29 years from discovery to production, second-slowest of 26 countries in S&P's 2024 study. Permitting for a federal-land REE mine typically takes multiple years even with FAST-41: Bear Lodge's FAST-41 schedule is 2 years (Mar 2026 → Mar 2028) for NEPA and permits, after a project history going back to the 2010s. US REE projects have historically stalled at the financing and processing stages, not only at permitting.

### Cited Findings
- S&P Global (Jul 2024), "Mine Development Times: The U.S. in Perspective": the US average from discovery to production is about 29 years, second only to Zambia (34). Canada is 27. 268 mines were studied. Only three US mines entered production since 2002, and ten projects have been in development for decades (one since 1978) — [S&P Global press release](https://press.spglobal.com/2024-07-18-United-States-Ranks-Next-to-Last-in-Development-Time-for-New-Mines-that-Produce-Critical-Minerals-for-Energy-Transition,-S-P-Global-Finds); [Northern Miner](https://northernminer.com/news/zambia-us-and-canada-slowest-for-mine-development-sp-global/1003869733)
- A separate S&P dataset (127 precious/base metal mines starting 2002–2023) gives a US average lead time of 19.1 years vs 15.5 globally. The 29-year figure includes undeveloped projects in the sample — [S&P Global MI](https://www.spglobal.com/marketintelligence/en/news-insights/research/discovery-to-production-averages-15-7-years-for-127-mines); [Mining Indaba](https://miningindaba.com/articles/how-isinnovation-reshapingexploration-anddeve)
- Bear Lodge: FAST-41 coverage on Mar 20, 2026, with a target completion of 03/20/2028 (USFS lead) — [Permitting.gov](https://www.permitting.gov/newsroom/press-releases/bear-lodge-rare-earth-project-latest-gain-fast-41-coverage). The mine target is 2030, and the project was previously permitted under NEPA in an earlier cycle — [RER presentation](https://rareelementresources.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Corporate-Presentation.pdf)
- Elk Creek: the 2026 FS follows "12 years of engineering, metallurgical testing and mine-planning," with a 35-month pre-production period after financing — [NioCorp FS webcast](https://www.niocorp.com/wp-content/uploads/08-11-2026_NioCorp_FSResults_Webcast_vFINAL.pdf). EXIM application first made in 2023 (up to $800M) and still not final in Oct 2026 — [Metal Tech News](https://www.metaltechnews.com/story/2026/10/21/tech-metals/exim-affirms-65-percent-elk-creek-financing-target/2988.html)
- Round Top: the resource was defined in 2012–13 ([TRER 8-K 2012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001445942/000114420412034981/v316166_ex99-1.htm)), with a production target of late 2028 ([USAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1970622/000197062226000019/exhibit991-earningsrelease.htm)), about 16 years
- FAST-41 expansion: 50 critical-mineral/mining projects covered by Nov 2025, 49 added since Jan 2025 under the EO on Immediate Measures to Increase American Mineral Production — [Permitting.gov](https://www.permitting.gov/newsroom/press-releases/permitting-council-meets-agency-milestone-50-mining-infrastructure-projects); latest additions in Jul 2026 — [Permitting.gov](https://www.permitting.gov/newsroom/press-releases/latest-group-transparency-projects-join-fast-41)
- Exploration on federal land can be reviewed by categorical exclusion (Sheep Creek, Jul 2026) after an EA was cancelled. Local and congressional opposition can still slow it — [Montana Free Press](https://montanafreepress.org/2026/07/27/forest-service-reviews-exploratory-mining-projects-in-the-headwaters-of-two-montana-rivers/)
- Mountain Pass history: concentrate continuously ≥40,000 t/yr since 2021 — [MP 10-K FY2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136824000014/mp-20231231.htm)

### Inferences
- Typical company-guided "build" periods (2.5 years Halleck, 35 months Elk Creek) are short relative to the pre-build phases: studies, permitting (2–4+ years) and financing (Elk Creek EXIM at 3+ years). Projects on state or private land (Halleck Creek on Wyoming state land, Round Top on Texas state land, Brook on private/coal-permitted land) avoid federal NEPA but still need state mining, air, water and radioactive-materials permits.
- For the report: assume first new US primary REE mine output from about 2028–2030 at the earliest, and meaningful volume (more than 10 kt REO/yr additional) after 2030.

### Gaps
- No systematic dataset of US REE-specific permitting durations was found. The examples above are project-specific.
